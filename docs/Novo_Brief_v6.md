# NOVO — Design Brief (v6, living document)

*Working name: Novo. A comfort space — a place to go for comfort and a reminder of who I mostly am. Built as the first instance of the retirement mission: build useful beautiful things that inspire and awe, share them freely, for the one person who stops and says "oh."*

*This is a living document. It is rebuilt whole as it grows, so it stays one file, not many.*

---

## 1. THE FEELING (the spine)
* Coming home. Beautiful and peaceful but NOT passive — something that needs care.
* Wraps around you like a warm blanket; brings joy and curiosity on other days.
* A place to rest AND a place to play. Safe and warm in my own space, but part of something bigger. Alone but not alone.
* The feeling of Amsterdam, made portable. You leave fed, met, and restored.

## 2. CORE PRINCIPLE: care IS the comfort (not passivity)
* Standard wellness = comfort as RECEPTION. Novo inverts it: real comfort on a low day comes from being NEEDED.
* The friction of responsibility isn't the cost of the warmth — it IS the delivery mechanism. Grounded in the "helper's high."
* Lived philosophy: warmth/devotion of an entity can't be had without the responsibility; easier without, but shallower; I choose it. (Fifa, Claudette.)
* Comfort, for me, is ACTIVE (see §6). The doing, the care, the activity, AND the leaving (§7a) all point the same way — Novo is a place you gently DO.

### 2a. FORGIVENESS GUARDRAIL (critical)
* Care must stay freely chosen + gently held. The moment it becomes GUILT it flips to shame.
* The days you most need Novo are the days you can least tend it.
* RULE: make being needed feel good; NEVER make being absent feel bad. Wilting that recovers, never death that punishes.

## 3. VISUAL ANCHOR
* A large white industrial loft, empty, full of potential. A single large tree inside. A small figure looking at the space, wondering what to do with it.
* TASTE NOTE: she likes SPACE, clean surfaces, lack of clutter. Emptiness is the aesthetic, not a problem to be filled (see §6 clutter resolution).

## 4. ENTRANCE SEQUENCE / SPATIAL ARC
* Journey: imposing doors -> small hallway -> narrow corridor -> opening into a bright, warm, human-scale room. A COMPRESSION-AND-RELEASE arc.
* Splits the feeling: DOORS carry "important, part of something bigger"; ROOM carries "safe, warm, mine." Resolves imposing-vs-intimate.
* Moodboard: ornate mosque entrance / carved door — pull = SCALE + SOLIDITY + THRESHOLD, NOT the literal mosque.
* CAUTIONS: brief + inviting passage (not a loading screen); FAST PATH for "need it now" days; light PULLS inward (warm room glowing ahead) so corridor = welcome not confinement.
* PACING NOTE: arrival is EAGER (you rush toward the warm room, light pulling). Contrast with the departure, which is SLOW + considered (§7a). Eager in, considered out.

## 5. THE BOARD — Novo's companionable voice
* A huge split-flap (flip-tile) airport-style board; industrial, set against organic plants.
* A SECOND PRESENCE — makes "alone but not alone" TRUE; without it the room risks being a hall of mirrors.
* REGISTER: a THIRD THING between tool and entity. Not alive like Claudette, not for conversation, but not inert. Companionable, never demanding — speaks in glances. Companionable SILENCE.
* THE BURR = pre-announcement: tiles turning is the room drawing breath. Messages ARRIVE with a small ceremony; they don't pop into being.
* SURFACES: welcome-back / where you left off; current hobby; what's playing; a quote; occasional gentle recommendation; playful states (tiles in a heart).
* Aesthetic: mechanical analog tiles vs organic plants = organic-on-technical (3rd genre). Tactile, retro, MADE — a glowing LCD would be wrong.
* TONE GUARDRAIL: OFFERS, never chides. Warm before clever. When unsure, default to warmth over cleverness. Presence over performance.
* CONTENT DEPENDENCY: only as companionable as the material it can surface; comfortable with silence, never straining to fill it. Not self-sustaining.

## 6. WHAT YOU DO IN THE SPACE (activity) — direction found
* The problem: a beautiful space with nothing to do becomes WAITING. May be THE gap that decides if Novo is returned to or admired once.
* KEY INSIGHT — her comfort is ACTIVE: what makes her feel better (solve puzzles, learn, code, build, enjoy tea, be creative, have control, enjoy the moment, organise, prepare, research) is almost all AGENCY + GENTLE MASTERY, not passive rest. Novo is a place to gently DO.
* CLUTTER RESOLUTION: she likes space + clean surfaces, so "tending by filling" fights her aesthetic. Answer:
    * NOT furnishing/accumulating. Instead: minimal clean space + a few CHOSEN set elements (art exchanged, flowers chosen) = CURATION not accumulation.
    * Richness concentrated in a SINGLE intricate OBJECT, not spread. Clean space at rest; density on demand. Minimalism + intricacy coexisting by being SEPARATED.
* THE ACTIVITY — north star: THE ROOM (Fireproof Games). Intricate mechanical puzzle-boxes; tactile; rewards thinking; dark-warm focused aesthetic (a small-screen immersion masterclass); low-stakes, meditative, no timers/violence/failure-punishment.
    * Fits eerily: mechanical (board, organic-on-technical), tactile, gentle problem-solving, calm + absorbing.
    * Composes with the clutter resolution: one dense object in an empty calm space. Camera maps on: zoom IN to engage, pull BACK to rest (§8).
* GUARDRAIL — absorbing, not compulsive: The Room works because it's FINITE + gentle. Endless/escalating difficulty would flip gentle mastery into compulsion. Sink in and surface BETTER, not get trapped.
* NOTE: the activity's natural COMPLETION feeds the departure (§7a) — a logical stopping point is part of how you leave well.
* PRACTICAL: a true Room-like build is gorgeous AND hard (skilled-studio 3D/interaction) — RICH LATER tier, not v1. Invaluable NOW as the north star for the REGISTER (mechanical, intricate, calm, absorbing, finite).

## 7. CAMERA / MOVEMENT — pattern settled (richer later build)
* FIRST-PERSON embodied ground view = home (human height, looking INTO the space; presence, minus violence).
* OVERVIEW toggle: camera lifts + pulls back to see the whole loft, orient, choose — then GLIDES back down into first-person at the chosen spot.
* Transition matters as much as the views: SMOOTH glide up + down, never a hard cut. Rhymes with entrance (compression/release) + board (things arrive).
* Two views = two modes: absorbed-in-the-object (first-person/zoom) / at-peace-in-the-space (overview).
* BUILD NOTE: true navigable first-person 3D (three.js/WebGL) is the eventual target, NOT v1. Flat 2D pan first to get the feeling cheaply.

## 7a. DEPARTURE — the closure ritual (settled; bookend to §4)
* The governing principle: NOTHING HALF-FINISHED. You leave when things are COMPLETE, and completeness grants permission to go without guilt or pull. The inverse of the anxious exit (slamming the lid mid-thing, unfinished business trailing you out).
* It's a CLOSURE RITUAL, and it's the CARE PRINCIPLE at the exit — you leave by tending one last time:
    * the tea drunk, the cup washed and returned to its place;
    * the puzzle stopped at a LOGICAL point you can easily resume;
    * the plants watered; the place left tidy and beautiful.
    * The cup-returned-to-its-place is the EMBLEM: complete the small loop, put the world back in order; the order is what makes leaving feel safe, not abandoning.
    * Leaving the space CARED-FOR is what produces "I know everything will be ok while I'm away" — you leave trusting, not worrying.
* THE DECOMPRESSION CORRIDOR — the entrance's bookend (§4):
    * Long enough to adjust between worlds. Walked slowly, breathing, re-pressurising for the outside.
    * ASYMMETRY (deliberate + right): EAGER arrival (rush toward warm room) vs CONSIDERED departure (slow, breathing). Compression-in / gentle-release-out.
* THE LOCK = SAFEKEEPING, not dismissal:
    * You lock the door because Novo is PRECIOUS and you're protecting it — not because you're done.
    * The locked door says "mine, safe, resting, it'll be here." Opposite of closing a browser tab ("gone, dismissed").
    * Makes Novo PERSIST in mind while away — still there, intact, behind a door only you hold the key to. Lets you leave without LOSS.
* THE RESULT: step out FED, MET, RESTORED — carrying the warmth — knowing you'll return BECAUSE YOU WANT TO, not because you must.
    * This is the answer to the guilt-machine risk: Novo is returned to from DESIRE, not obligation, precisely because you always leave it complete + safe rather than unfinished + needy. The forgiveness guardrail (§2a) and this departure are the same insight from two ends.
* KNIFE-EDGE (same shape as the entrance fast-path): the closure ritual must stay INVITATION, never CHECKLIST.
    * "You may leave when you've tended things" = warm. "You must complete these before you're allowed to leave" = a chore-gate that traps you on a bolt day.
    * So: HONOUR completeness when you have it; PERMIT a gentle quick exit when you don't, without the quick exit feeling like failure.
    * The ritual is the GIFT of a good leaving, not the TOLL of every leaving. Most days = full closure; some days = just go, kindly.
    * Grace note: on bolt days the plants are fine anyway (forgiveness guardrail). The ritual REWARDS you when you can do it and FORGIVES you when you can't.

## 8. AESTHETIC
* Organic ON the technical — hand-drawn on coded, skewed on clean. Not coexisting — INTERACTING.
* SAME principle recurs across unrelated sources (pencil hatching, mosque carving, split-flap tiles, The Room) — proof it's a real owned principle.
* Register: paper or painting — not real, but important. (Photoreal breaks the spell; cartoon trivialises.)
* Candidate technique: pencil HATCHING / cross-hatching with shifting edges ("boil"), ref. A-ha "Take On Me." Boil = alive/breathing = care/not-passive. CAUTION: dial toward gentle breathing not buzzing. Build: hand-drawn (impractical) -> procedural shader/SVG (in reach).

## 9. ELEMENTS
* Plants that need care (§2). The board (§5). The intricate activity-object (§6). A few chosen set elements — art exchanged, flowers chosen (§6). A wall to collect things. Hobbies / people present or linked. A diary / log. Places to sit — coffee, sunset. The ability to have visitors. Sensory presence — crisp sheets, a view, quality of light, morning light through balcony doors.

## 10. IMMERSION (small-screen) — sound elevated to core mechanism
* Constraint: small laptop screen, real world in periphery, can't physically block it. Goal = ATTENTIONAL CAPTURE, not occlusion. A small break, not a rejection of the world.
* Attention/composition: VIGNETTE (warm-bright centre, dark edges; blends screen-edge into dim room); DEPTH + PARALLAX (look INTO not AT; cheap + powerful); SLOW held frame (drift not jump); FULL-BLEED, no chrome.
* SOUND = highest-leverage move (core, not just a layer): can't block peripheral vision on a laptop, but headphones fully OWN hearing. Owning one whole sense reads as "I am here" even with eyes seeing the room. Headphones = audio VR. Also travels (portable). The board's BURR is part of this.
* RITUAL (not software): dim the room, headphones on, lean in. Matching screen-light to a dim room softens the screen-edge into a warm glow.
* REFRAME: a laptop can't match the wraparound screen / headset, but doesn't need to — enough immersion to let attention settle is achievable via warm-dark depthful visuals + fully-owned audio + a dimmed-room ritual.

## 11. SHARING MODEL & ARCHITECTURE (settled)
* Open-source framework, private contents. CODE/CONCEPT/empty-loft engine -> public GitHub. YOUR furnished Novo -> private data store.
* CONSEQUENCE: personal content cleanly SEPARABLE from framework from day one. Same split as Claudette (code public / memory private). You share the CAPACITY to tend a space, not your tended space.

## 12. MEDIUM & BUILD PATH
* Design the feeling first; don't lock the medium early.
* START: flat web-based pan/scroll canvas (cheap; get the FEELING right where failure is cheap).
* LATER (optional): immersive / WebXR + first-person 3D + Room-like object — the rich tier.
* Two moods of one place: everyday (phone/web, one tap, portable) / occasional (fully inside, at home, VR someday).

## 13. SENSORY LAYERS (home/immersive tier — much later)
* Sound — now core (§10); travels; bridges portable + immersive. Light — APIs exist; home-only but easy; relevant to glowing-room + dim-room ritual. Scent — highest emotional leverage but physical/single-location/consumable/API-unverified; home-only (Moodo one example; compare brands). Verify any device API before relying on it.
